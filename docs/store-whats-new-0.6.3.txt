This update brings everything since 0.5.1 to the App Store.

Lower memory use
klipa no longer keeps whole images in memory to draw the small previews
in the dropdown. Its footprint stays small no matter how large the
screenshots in your history are.

Honest keep-awake
Keep-awake reports what it is actually doing rather than assuming a
request worked: preparing, active, recovering, or needs attention. When
macOS will not keep your Mac awake in a given configuration, klipa says
so and names the reason instead of failing quietly. Timed sessions end
on their own, and changing the length or mode of a running session no
longer drops protection for a moment.

Clearer about closing the lid
klipa now tells you what closing the lid will actually do on your Mac.
With an external display attached it stays awake with no special
privilege. On a bare laptop that needs a system-level override, which
the App Store version is sandboxed and cannot perform, so klipa says
that plainly rather than appearing to work.

Also in this update
Image entries show a preview thumbnail in the dropdown. The menu bar can
show the icon, the date, the temperature, or a combination. The dropdown
stays a compact panel instead of growing to fill the screen. Various
reliability fixes to keep-awake and to how history is stored.
